## Notes and Observations

* **Texture and Flavor:** The preparation came out nicely. It is somewhat crunchy, picklish, and acetic, which is consistent with expectations.
* **Serving Temperature and Aging:** It tastes much better when served cold. The flavor develops and improves after a few days of aging in the fridge.
* **Potential Improvements:** The profile could definitely benefit from the addition of aromatics or a brighter acidity.
