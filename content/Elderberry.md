*Sambucus canadensis*

Elderberries are small, deeply colored, tart fruits generally used cooked in syrups, jams, pies, wine, and cordials.[^1]

### Useful facts

- Do not eat the berries or their juice raw: they contain cyanogenic glycosides. Cooking reduces these compounds and makes the fruit and juice safe when a tested recipe is followed.[^2]
- Fresh, frozen, and dried berries can all be used for cooked preparations.[^2]

### Northeast notes

American elder is native to Maine and tolerates a wide range of soils, including wet sites. UMaine identifies it as a practical native berry shrub for Maine gardens and culinary use.[^1][^3]
