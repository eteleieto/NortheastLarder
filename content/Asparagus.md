*Asparagus officinalis*

Asparagus is a perennial vegetable grown for its young shoots, called spears. The harvested spear is an immature fern and may be eaten raw or cooked.[^1]

### History

Asparagus originated in Asia Minor and has been cultivated for culinary and medicinal uses for more than 2,000 years.[^1]

### Northeast notes

In Maine, asparagus is a popular early-spring crop; harvest in central Maine typically begins in early May. Productive beds require time to establish: UMaine advises delaying harvest until the second year for crown-planted beds and the third for seedling-grown beds.[^1]
