## Formula

* **Salt:** 2%

## Notes and Observations

* **Fermentation Speed:** Fermented incredibly fast. After only a few days, the batch was already highly acidic.
* **Flavor Profile:** Tasty, with a profile leaning more toward a pickle-like, acetic character. 
* **Acidity:** The acidity is slightly jarring, likely because it fermented too quickly. 

## Future Adjustments

* **Fermentation Time:** Need to test and calibrate the fermentation time to control the rapid acid development.
* **Applications:** While sharp on its own, this batch could be put to excellent use as an ingredient in other preparations.
