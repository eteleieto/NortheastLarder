*Arctium lappa* and *Arctium minus*

Burdock is a Eurasian biennial now common in disturbed ground around the Northeast. The long first-year root is the main food part; cultivated great burdock is the vegetable known in Japan as gobo.[^1]

### Harvest it before the second year

The plant spends its first year as a low rosette and sends up its tall flowering stalk in the second. Besides the root, young leaves and immature flower stalks are edible before they become coarse.[^1]

### The burr behind Velcro

Burdock burrs travel by catching their tiny hooks on fur and clothing. After examining burrs caught on his dog, Swiss inventor George de Mestral used the same hook-and-loop idea for Velcro.[^2]

### Root, leaf, and shoot

Burdock and [[Dandelion 1|dandelion]] are both members of the aster family and both have food traditions centered on roots as well as leaves or shoots.
