## Process

1. Cook soaked [[Soybean|soybeans]] in a covered hotel pan with 1–2 inches of liquid above the beans.
2. After cooking, rest in the fridge overnight.

## Notes and Observations

* Once chilled, the cooking liquid (aquasoy) should be thick, similar to egg whites.
* It whips well—though not quite to stiff peaks—and is useful for aerating cakes or other preparations that need to trap air.
