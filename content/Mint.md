*Mentha* spp.

Mint is a bright, cooling herb used fresh in salads, sauces, drinks, teas, and desserts, or dried for a deeper, more compact flavor. Spearmint and peppermint are common culinary types; choose by taste rather than relying on the broad name alone.[^1]

### Kitchen notes

- Add fresh mint late in cooking to preserve its aroma; muddle, chop, steep, or blend it according to the preparation.
- Dried mint is much stronger than fresh: UMaine recommends using roughly one-quarter to one-third of the fresh amount called for in a recipe.[^2]

### Northeast notes

Mint is a perennial culinary herb that can be grown outdoors in the Northeast and divided from established garden plants for indoor winter use.[^1][^3]
