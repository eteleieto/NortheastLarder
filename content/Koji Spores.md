Koji spores, or *koji tane*, are the inoculum used to begin a fresh batch of [[Koji|koji]]. They are sold either as a concentrated powder or on dried grains of rice or barley; both forms can inoculate a different cooked-grain substrate.[^1]

For every 1 g of powdered spores, mix with 9 g of toasted flour or starch. The carrier makes the tiny quantity easier to distribute evenly with a shaker or sieve; a little inoculum goes a long way.[^1]

[^1]: Rene Redzepi and David Zilber, *The Noma Guide to Fermentation* (Artisan, 2018), PDF p. 365.
