*Pisum sativum*

Peas range from sweet shelling peas to crisp snap peas and starchy dried peas. They are all forms of the same cool-weather crop, but with very different textures.[^1]


In Maine, peas are among the crops sown as soon as the ground can be worked, making them one of the first annual food plants into the garden.[^2]

Fresh peas remain metabolically active after picking. They respire rapidly, consuming sugars within hours, while some of the remaining sugar is converted to starch. The familiar decline from tender sweetness toward mealiness is therefore a continuing biological change, not simply moisture loss.[^3]
