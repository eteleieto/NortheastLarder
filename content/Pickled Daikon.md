## Background

The goal of this experiment is to prepare a pickled [[Daikon|daikon]] infused with mint, aiming for a profile similar to Korean yellow pickled radish (danmuji). 

## Recipe

### Ingredients

* 1 [[Daikon|daikon]] radish
* Fresh mint
* **Brine:**
    * 200 g apple cider vinegar
    * 200 g water
    * 50 g [[Sorghum 1|sorghum molasses]]
    * 25 g salt

## Notes and Observations

* The salt quantity was adjusted during preparation from 20 g, briefly to 30 g, before settling on a final weight of 25 g.
* **Tasting Notes:** The result is pleasant, but it could use a bit more sweetener in the next batch to closer approximate the traditional sweetness of Korean yellow pickled daikon.

## Future Adjustments

* Increase the amount of [[Sorghum 1|sorghum molasses]] or add another sweetener in the next trial to boost the sweetness.
