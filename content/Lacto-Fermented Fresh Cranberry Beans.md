## Background

Testing a simple [[Lacto-Fermentation]] on fresh [[Cranberry Bean|cranberry beans]]. 

## Process

### Ingredients
- Fresh [[Cranberry Bean|cranberry beans]]
- Salt (for boiling water and 2% by weight for the ferment)
- Water

### Method
1. Boil the fresh cranberry beans for approximately 5 minutes in water with a few percent of salt to remove residual starch.
2. Drain the beans and place them into a jar.
3. Add water and salt at a ratio of 2% total weight.

## Notes and Observations

- **Texture:** The beans needed to be cooked more thoroughly before going into the jar; the 5-minute boil was insufficient.
