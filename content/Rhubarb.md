*Rheum × hybridum*

Rhubarb is one of the first local tart ingredients of the year. The stalks break down quickly into sauce, pie filling, jam, or a sharp savory glaze; strawberries, ginger, cream, and warm spices all make sense with it.[^1]

The simple rule: use the stalks and discard the leaves. Rhubarb leaves are poisonous.[^1]

Rhubarb thrives in the northern states and is a very familiar Maine backyard crop. Fresh stalks keep for about a week in the refrigerator; beyond that, freeze or preserve them.[^1]
