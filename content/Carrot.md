*Daucus carota* subsp. *sativus*

Carrots are taproots selected into a wide range of shapes and colors. Their roots hold well after harvest and range from orange to purple, red, yellow, and white.[^1]

### Orange is only one pigment story

Orange carrots get their color from beta-carotene; purple carrots instead have anthocyanin pigments.[^2]

### A crop for the cool edges of the season

Carrots can be sown in Maine in spring and again later in the season; their cool-weather tolerance is part of why they fit the region so well.[^3]
