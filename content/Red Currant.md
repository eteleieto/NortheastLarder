*Ribes rubrum* group

Red currants are translucent, sharply acidic berries carried in dangling clusters. They belong to *Ribes*, the same genus as black currants and gooseberries.

### The fruit caught in a pine-disease quarantine

Currants have an unusual legal and ecological history in Maine. *Ribes* plants are part of the life cycle of white pine blister rust, so planting and possession are restricted across many Maine counties and towns.[^1]

### The law is about rust, not currants

The quarantine is not about the fruit itself. It exists because the rust alternates between *Ribes* and five-needle pines, including eastern white pine.[^1]

[^1]: University of Maine Cooperative Extension, “[Are Red Currant Plants Allowed in Maine?](https://extension.umaine.edu/gardening/2024/05/24/are-red-currant-plants-allowed-in-maine/),” 2024.
