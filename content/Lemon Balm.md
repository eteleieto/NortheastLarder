*Melissa officinalis*

Lemon balm is a softly lemon-scented herb, best used fresh in cold drinks, fruit, soft desserts, and gentle infusions. Its flavor is less sharp than lemon peel or juice, so it works as an aromatic rather than an acid.[^1]

### Kitchen notes

- Use fresh leaves for the clearest aroma; steep rather than boil aggressively when making an infusion.
- It dries well only when handled promptly: lemon balm has high moisture content and can mold if not dried quickly.[^2]

### Northeast notes

Lemon balm is among the culinary herbs that can be grown outside in the Northeast. For winter use in Maine, UMaine recommends starting it in pots from seed.[^1][^3]
