## Background

Tempeh is a traditional Indonesian food made from soybeans bound together in the firm mycelium of the mold *Rhizopus oligosporus*. Thanks to its fermentation process, it has a nutty, earthy flavor and a dense texture. It can, however, be grown on practically anything.
