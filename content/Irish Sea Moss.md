*Chondrus crispus*

Irish sea moss is a red seaweed used for its gentle marine flavor and natural thickening power. In the kitchen, it is best known for setting or giving body to puddings, broths, desserts, and other soft preparations.[^1]


- Rinse thoroughly, soak if dried, then simmer or blend according to the recipe; a little goes a long way.
- Treat it as a textural ingredient first, with its marine flavor used in balance with dairy, fruit, or savory stocks.


Irish moss is a Maine sea vegetable and is cultured by approved land-based aquaculture facilities in the state. UMaine specifically lists it as a seaweed used in pudding.[^1][^2]
