*Triticum spelta*

Spelt is a hulled wheat, closely related to common wheat, used as whole berries, flour, malt, and in fermented grain preparations. It has a nutty flavor and must be dehulled before it is ready for human food.[^1]

### History

Spelt is one of the earliest domesticated grains. It is also known as dinkel in Germany and Austria and, in Italy, as farro.[^1]

### Northeast notes

UVM Extension evaluates heirloom winter-spelt varieties in Vermont and identifies it as a potential specialty grain crop for Northeast farmers. Dehulling adds a processing step and should be considered alongside local milling and market access.[^1][^2]
