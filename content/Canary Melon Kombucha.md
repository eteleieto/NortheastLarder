## Formula

### Ingredients
- 500 g [[Canary Melon|canary melon]] trim and guts
- 15% [[Kombucha|kombucha starter]]
- 8% honey
- Water to balance

## Notes and Observations
- Fermented for 5 days at room temperature.
- The final result was clean and refreshing, with a subtle but definitely noticeable cantaloupe flavor.
- When combined with [[Lacto-Strawberries]], it was quite delicious — definitely an interesting base for a drink with a fruitiness that utilizes byproduct.
