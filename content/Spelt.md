*Triticum spelta*

Spelt is a hulled wheat: the husk stays tightly attached after threshing and requires an extra dehulling step. It contains gluten and is not a wheat-free grain.

### Winter spelt under trial

UMaine trials found meaningful differences among winter spelt varieties in height, lodging, yield, and protein. Maine farms have successfully grown winter spelt for both grain and straw.[^1]

### Planting later shifts yield and protein

In the UMaine trial, later planting reduced yield but tended to raise grain protein. That tradeoff makes planting date part of the ingredient’s eventual composition, not merely a farm-calendar detail.[^1]

### A hulled wheat with gluten

Spelt belongs in the same genus as [[Wheat|wheat]] and shares its gluten-forming proteins.
