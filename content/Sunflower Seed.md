*Helianthus annuus*

The edible seed of the annual sunflower, eaten whole or pressed for oil. Oilseed and edible-seed types are distinct practical choices for growers and cooks.[^1]

### Useful facts

- The visible sunflower head is an inflorescence: its central disk flowers each develop a seed after fertilization.[^2]
- Seeds are ready to harvest after the petals wither and the seeds are visible.[^1]

### Northeast notes

In Maine, sunflowers are typically direct-sown after the last frost. UMaine Extension reports 50–95 days to maturity depending on variety, and field trials found potential for oilseed-sunflower production on a farm scale.[^1][^3]
