## Formula
- 100g [[Oyster Mushroom|oyster mushrooms]]
- 250g water (added as the mushrooms did not release sufficient liquid)
- 2% salt

## Process
1. Freeze mushrooms, then thaw to draw out water.
2. Combine mushrooms and water, add 2% salt, and weigh down to keep submerged.

## Notes and Observations
- Freezing and thawing did not draw out much moisture from the oyster mushrooms, though the method worked well previously on [[Cremini Mushroom|creminis]]. Added water was necessary to ensure adequate coverage.
- The final flavor profile is distinctly cheese-like, almost parmesan-like, and quite strong.
