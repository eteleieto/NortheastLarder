## Background
The goal of this experiment is to ferment [[Oyster Mushroom|oyster mushrooms]] using a dry-salting method to draw out moisture similar to kimchi, utilizing previously fermented garlic to help inoculate the batch with active microbes. Smoked Hungarian wax pepper is used as a local (albeit non-similar) substitute for gochugaru.

## Recipe

### Ingredients
* 800 g [[Oyster Mushroom|oyster mushrooms]]
* 50 g fermented fresh garlic (from a previous active ferment)
* 100 g fresh onion
* 40 g dried, smoked [[Hungarian Wax Pepper|Hungarian wax pepper]] (as a gochugaru substitute)
* Salt (calculated at 2% of total weight for both the mushrooms and the paste mixture)

## Process

### Preparation
1. **Mushroom Prep:** Toss the 800 g of oyster mushrooms with 2% salt by weight (16 g) to draw out the moisture.
2. **Paste Prep:** Blend the fermented garlic, fresh onion, and smoked Hungarian wax pepper into a paste. 
3. **Salting the Paste:** Weigh the paste mixture and add 2% salt by weight.
4. **Inoculation & Fermentation:** Combine the salted mushrooms with the paste, ensuring the active microbes from the fermented garlic are well distributed. Pack into a fermentation vessel.

## Notes and Observations
* **Fermentation Speed:** The batch fermented incredibly quickly and became very sour in a short amount of time. In future trials, the fermentation should be stopped much earlier to control the acidity.
* **Flavor Profile:** The overall flavor is pleasant. The smoked [[Hungarian Wax Pepper|Hungarian wax peppers]] worked really well as a gochugaru alternative, providing a great smoky depth.

## Future Adjustments
* Pull the ferment earlier to prevent it from getting too sour too fast.
