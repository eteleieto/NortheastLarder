Blended celtuce leaves with a small amount of white vinegar to help slow oxidation and 5% water. After straining, the resulting juice still oxidized relatively quickly.

The result is grassy with notes of [[Apple|apple]].

Celtuce is a stem form of cultivated lettuce, *Lactuca sativa*, selected for its enlarged crisp stalk rather than a leafy head. Like other lettuces, it exudes white latex when freshly cut. The stalk is the characteristic food part, while the leaves remain usable but are secondary to it.[^1]

[^1]: Harold McGee, *On Food and Cooking: The Science and Lore of the Kitchen*, rev. ed. (Scribner, 2004), PDF p. 333.
