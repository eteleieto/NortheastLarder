*Arachis hypogaea*

Peanut is a legume, not a tree nut. Its pods develop underground, and the seeds are used whole, as a paste, and for oil.[^1]

### History

Cultivated peanut originated in South America. USDA research places its likely center of origin between southern Bolivia and northwestern Argentina, supported by the distribution of its two progenitor species and archaeological evidence.[^2]

### Useful facts

- Peanuts belong to the legume family, alongside peas and beans.[^1]
- Their geocarpic fruiting habit—pods maturing below ground—is unusual among food legumes.[^2]
