## Process

### Ingredients
- 320 g raw [[Oyster Mushroom|oyster mushrooms]]
- 300 g water
- 300 g apple cider vinegar
- 50 g [[Apple Cider Syrup|apple cider syrup]]
- 30 g salt

## Instructions
1. Combine the water, apple cider vinegar, apple cider syrup, and salt in a pot.
2. Add the oyster mushrooms.
3. Bring to a boil and simmer until the liquid is incorporated and the mushrooms are thoroughly softened.

## Notes and Observations
- The final result is very pleasant. 
- The cooking liquid is delicious, and the mushrooms themselves are highly flavorful.
