## Formula

### Ingredients
* 100g [[Koji|rice koji]]
* 100g light toasted [[Rye|rye]]
* 800g water
* 200g [[Jun|jun]] starter
* 8% honey

## Notes and Observations

* **Tasting Notes:** The flavor profile is reminiscent of a pleasant [[Amazake|amazake]]-like taste or a sour rice wine. It is generally pleasant, though currently a bit too sour and somewhat too funky. 

## Future Adjustments

* The funkiness and acidity could be mellowed out in the next trial by reducing the amount of [[Koji|koji]] used in the initial mix.
