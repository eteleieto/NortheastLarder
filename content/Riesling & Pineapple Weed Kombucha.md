## Formula

### Ingredients
* 675 g [[Grape|riesling grape]] juice
* 325 g water
* Few grams dried [[Pineapple Weed|pineapple weed]]

## Notes and Observations
* **Tasting Notes:** The combination of Riesling and pineapple weed is pleasant, and both flavors are present, but they are experienced at the exact same time. Without a separation of the fruitiness and the herbal aroma over time, the preparation lacks complexity and feels too one-note.
